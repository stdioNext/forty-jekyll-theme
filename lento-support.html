Common questions

A shield doesn’t appear, or an app doesn’t lock again

Open Lento’s Profile and check that Screen Time reads Allowed. Lento relies on Apple’s Screen Time; if Lento’s access has been taken away in Settings, under Screen Time, it can’t look after your apps. If a session ran out while your phone was asleep, the app locks again the next time you use your phone, or as soon as you open Lento.

Breathe on the shield doesn’t open Lento

On iOS 26.5 and later, Breathe opens Lento straight into the breath. On earlier versions, a “Take a breath” notification appears instead: tap it. If nothing appears, allow notifications for Lento in Settings.

How do I change an app’s budget, or stop guarding it?

On Home, tap the app and choose Settings. You can rename it and change its daily opens, session lengths and breath, then Save. Remove from Lento takes its shield away.

Managing or cancelling Lento Pro

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone go to Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Lento, or use Manage subscription in Lento’s Profile. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

Restoring a purchase

On a new iPhone, open Lento’s Profile and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.

Turning off analytics

In Lento’s Profile, switch off Share anonymous usage data. Lento then stops sending usage events; your Screen Time data never leaves your iPhone either way.

Deleting your data

Everything Lento keeps lives on your iPhone, so deleting the app deletes it. To have your subscription and analytics records deleted too, email us with your Support ID.
