Getting your phone back

This page exists so you can turn off call forwarding without the Rosa app — if you have deleted it, cancelled your subscription, changed phone, or simply cannot get to it.

Nothing on this page needs an account, and none of it involves us. Call forwarding is a setting on your mobile network, not inside Rosa. That means only you can remove it, and it also means it keeps working whatever happens to us.

Turn off all call forwarding

From your phone's normal dialler, call:

##002#

That is two hashes, 002, one hash. It clears every forwarding rule on your number and restores your network's own voicemail.

You should see a confirmation from your network within a few seconds. If you do not, wait a minute and dial it again — the code is handled by your network, not by your phone, so a poor signal can stop it registering.

This works whether or not you still pay us, and whether or not Rosa still exists.

Sending calls to Rosa again

Only useful if you are still a customer. Rosa's own Phone Setup screen gives you the exact code for your number, because it contains the Rosa number assigned to you — which is specific to your business, so it is not printed here.

The shape of it is:

61*ROSA-NUMBERSECONDS#

61 means "forward when I do not answer", and SECONDS is how long your phone rings before Rosa picks up. Do not guess the Rosa number. Forwarding your calls to a number that is not yours sends your customers to a stranger.

If the code does not work

  • Some networks disable these codes, especially on business or reseller tariffs. Contact your network and ask them to remove "call forward on no reply" from your line.
  • Dual-SIM phones register forwarding per SIM. Make sure the dialler is using the same SIM the forwarding was set on.
  • Wi-Fi calling can interfere. Turn it off, dial the code, turn it back on.

One thing worth knowing about cost

While forwarding is on, a diverted call is a call your phone makes to Rosa's number. Most UK contracts take that out of your inclusive minutes and it costs nothing extra. On some pay-as-you-go tariffs it is charged per minute, which can add up quickly. If you are on PAYG, check with your network before leaving forwarding on all day.
