8. Call forwarding on your own mobile

This is the part of Rosa that touches your own phone, so read it carefully.

For Rosa to answer calls you don't pick up, conditional call forwarding must be registered on your mobile. That is a setting on your mobile network's system, not on Rosa's and not on Apple's.

Apple does not allow an app to dial the codes that register forwarding. So the App shows you the code and copies it to your clipboard, and you dial it yourself. Rosa can then place a test call to confirm it worked.

By registering forwarding, you are giving an instruction to your own mobile network. You are responsible for that instruction.

8.2 Your network may charge you for forwarded calls

When a call is forwarded from your mobile to your Rosa number, your mobile network may charge you for that forwarded leg of the call. This charge is between you and your network. It is not included in your Rosa subscription, we do not receive any of it, we cannot see it, and we cannot control it or predict it.

Major UK networks do charge for forwarded calls on at least some tariffs. Check your own tariff before you set forwarding up. If you make a lot of forwarded calls on a tariff that charges for them, the cost can be significant, and it is yours to pay.

8.3 Forwarding replaces your carrier voicemail

Your network's voicemail is itself a form of conditional forwarding. Registering forwarding to Rosa will normally replace it. While Rosa forwarding is active, callers who don't reach you will get Rosa instead of your usual voicemail, and messages left in the ordinary way may no longer reach you.

8.4 We cannot control what your network does

Mobile networks differ, and they change. We cannot promise that:

  • calls forwarded to Rosa will arrive with the original caller's number attached — some networks strip or alter this, and where it is stripped Rosa will have less to work with.

If forwarding stops working, Rosa may stop receiving your calls without either of us being told. Check the forwarding status shown in the App from time to time and use the test-call feature if in doubt.

8.5 Removing forwarding is your responsibility

If you stop using Rosa, you must remove the forwarding from your mobile yourself. Cancelling your subscription, deleting your account or deleting the App does not remove it. Forwarding lives on your network, and it will stay there until it is cleared.

To make this as easy as possible, the App includes a prominent control — "Give me my phone back" — which is reachable in at most two taps, gives you the code to clear all forwarding (##002#), and walks you through restoring your network's voicemail.
