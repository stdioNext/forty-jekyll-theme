Yap — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 August 2026 Version: 1.0 Applies to: the Yap iOS application ("Yap: AI UGC Video") and the Yap service

1. The short version

Yap turns a product photo or a short product description into a vertical product video. You sign in with Apple, describe your product (or upload a photo of it), review an AI-assisted script, and Yap generates a 9:16 video with an AI presenter, narration and captions.

To do that, we process three kinds of information:

  1. Your account — the email address and name that Sign in with Apple provides, and an account identifier.
  2. Your content — the product descriptions and scripts you type, the product photos you upload, and the audio and video Yap generates for you. Your text and photos are sent to AI providers to generate your script and video. Section 5 explains exactly what goes where.
  3. Your purchases — which credit packs you bought, so we can credit your account. Apple handles the payment itself; we never see card details.

We do not sell personal data. We do not show advertising. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites — Yap contains no advertising identifier, no analytics SDK and no tracking of any kind. All AI presenters in Yap are fictional, computer-generated people; Yap does not clone voices and does not put your face, or anyone else's, into videos.

4. AI generation — what happens to your content

Yap is an AI product, and we would rather over-explain this than tuck it away.

  • When you describe a product, your description (and, if you attached one, your product photo) is sent to an AI language model, which writes the script. The same content is first checked by an AI moderation step, and the generated script is checked again before you see it. If moderation cannot say the content is safe, generation stops.
  • When you paste your own script, it is not sent to the script-writing AI at all. It is stored with your project so your scenes can be generated.
  • When Yap generates a video, the script text is turned into narration by a text-to-speech model, and a licensed, computer-generated presenter image is animated to speak it by a video model. Your product photo appears in the video where you choose.
  • Captions are timed from the script text itself — no speech-recognition service listens to anything.

What the AI providers receive: the text of your description or script, and — only when you attached one — temporary, expiring links to your product photo and to generated audio (these links expire within one to three minutes). Your name, email address and account identity are not sent to AI providers as part of generation requests.

What we do not do: we do not use your content to train AI models, and we do not permit our providers to use it for training under the terms we contract on. Yap has no voice cloning and no user-face avatars; every presenter is a fictional, AI-generated person.

AI output can be wrong. Scripts and videos are machine-generated; review them before you publish them anywhere. Free-tier exports carry a visible "Yap • AI generated" watermark.

5.1 Transfers outside the UK

Some of your content is processed outside the United Kingdom — in particular, AI generation runs through a US-headquartered platform. Your account data and stored content live in London, but the text you submit for script generation, and expiring links to your product photo and generated audio, transit AI providers that may process them in the United States.

Where data leaves the UK, we rely on the transfer mechanisms permitted by UK GDPR — the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses with the UK Addendum, or an adequacy decision (including the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the supplier is certified).

7. Security

  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.
  • Photos and generated media are held in private storage that is not publicly reachable; the app reads them through short-lived signed links.
  • Access to your data is enforced at the database level with row-level security — one account cannot read another's projects, photos or credits.
  • The app bundle contains no secret keys; AI-provider and payment keys live only on our servers, and the app never talks to AI providers directly.
  • Server logs exclude prompts, photos, tokens and signed links; we log correlation IDs, not content.
  • Credit balances are kept in an append-only ledger so purchases and refunds are applied exactly once.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not claim otherwise. If you think you have found a security problem in Yap, email mert@studionext.co.uk and we will take it seriously.

If there is a data breach likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will report it to the ICO within 72 hours of becoming aware, as required by Art. 33 UK GDPR, and if it is likely to result in a high risk to you we will tell you directly and without undue delay.

8. Your responsibilities for what you upload

Only upload product photos you have the right to use, and only publish generated videos in line with the platforms you post them to. Do not upload photos of identifiable people. Yap's moderation is automated and probabilistic — it is a safety net, not a substitute for your own judgement about your content.

9. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection, and rights about automated decisions. Yap's only automated decision is content moderation of what you submit for generation; if your content is rejected and you believe that is wrong, email us and a human will look at it.

To exercise any right, email mert@studionext.co.uk. We respond within one month, extendable by up to two further months for complex requests (we will tell you if so). We will not charge you unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive. We may need to verify you control the account before acting.

Complaining to the regulator. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office — Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF — helpline 0303 123 1113 — ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. You do not have to come to us first, though we would like the chance to put it right.
