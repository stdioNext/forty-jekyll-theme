Rosa

Rosa — the receptionist for people with both hands full

You're up a ladder, under a sink, driving, or stood in front of a customer. The phone rings. By the time you can get to it, they've rung the next name on the list.

Rosa answers instead.

How it works

You get your own UK number. Keep the mobile number you've always had — forwarding sends on only the calls you don't pick up yourself, and nothing else about your phone changes.

Rosa has a real conversation. No "press 1 for". It answers in your business name, asks the questions you'd ask — what's the job, where are you, when do you need it — and listens to the answers.

The job lands on your phone. Who rang, what they want, how urgent it is, and a button to ring them straight back. You read it in ten seconds between jobs.

You can turn it off whenever you want. It's your phone. Rosa shows you the code that hands it back, and that page works whether or not you're a customer: getting your phone back.

Straight with the people who ring you

Rosa opens every call by saying that it's an AI assistant and that the call is recorded. That is not a setting a business owner can switch off. Your callers have a right to know who they're talking to, and in the UK that's the law rather than a preference.

Before Rosa answers a single call, the app shows you every company involved in handling it and exactly what each one receives. You can withdraw that permission at any time, and Rosa stops answering.

Plumbers, electricians, builders, roofers, gardeners, cleaners, barbers, salons, driving instructors — anyone running a business where the phone rings while both hands are full.
