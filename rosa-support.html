Turning Rosa off

Rosa answers your calls because your mobile network is forwarding them, and that setting lives on the network rather than in the app. Deleting the app does not switch it off.

To put your phone back exactly as it was, dial ##002# and press call. That clears every kind of forwarding on the line and restores your carrier voicemail. There is a full guide, including what to do when your network blocks the code, at studionext.co.uk/rosa-forwarding.html.

The same instructions are in the app under Business → Phone setup → Give me my phone back. They work whether or not you're signed in and whether or not you have a subscription.

Common questions

Do I keep my own number? Yes. Nobody needs to learn a new number for you. Forwarding sends on only the calls you don't answer yourself.

What do my callers hear? Rosa opens every call by saying that it's an AI assistant and that the call is recorded. That announcement can't be switched off — the people ringing you have a right to know who they're speaking to.

Does forwarding cost me anything? While forwarding is on, a diverted call is a call your phone makes to Rosa's number. Most UK monthly contracts take that out of your inclusive minutes at no extra cost. On some pay-as-you-go tariffs it's charged per minute, so it's worth a quick check with your network before leaving forwarding on all day.

What counts towards my monthly calls? An answered call is one Rosa picks up and holds a conversation on for 20 seconds or more. Calls under 20 seconds and calls detected as spam don't count. There's no per-minute charge and no overage charge — if you use up the month's allowance, Rosa keeps answering and takes a message until it resets.

How do I cancel? Subscriptions are handled by Apple, so cancelling happens in your Apple Account: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Rosa. In the app, Business → Manage your plan → Cancel or update payment takes you straight there.

How do I delete my account? Business → Delete account, three taps from opening the app. It removes your business profile, your calls, the recordings and transcripts, and releases your Rosa number. Clear your call forwarding first, or your phone will keep diverting to a number that no longer answers.
