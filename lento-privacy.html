lento Mindful Screen Time, by Studio Next

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026. Applies to the Lento app for iPhone.

Lento is built so that what you do on your phone stays on your phone. This policy explains exactly what Lento keeps, what it shares, and why.

In short

  • Your Screen Time data never leaves your iPhone. The apps you guard reach Lento as private tokens that neither Lento nor we can read.
  • No account, no ads and no tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • Two services see anything at all: RevenueCat, for Lento Pro subscriptions, and PostHog, for anonymous usage events that you can turn off in Profile.

Screen Time stays on your iPhone

Lento uses Apple’s Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity). When you choose apps for Lento to look after, iOS gives Lento opaque tokens instead of the apps’ names: Lento itself cannot tell which apps they are, and neither can we. Your app usage, the apps you choose and your Screen Time activity are processed by iOS on your device and are never sent to us.

Information kept only on your device

  • The first name you enter, so Lento can greet you.
  • Your settings: daily budgets, session lengths, breath lengths and how much you scroll today (the estimate you give during setup).
  • Your history in Lento: breathing sessions and opens per day.

This information lives in Lento’s storage on your iPhone. We don’t receive it. Deleting Lento deletes it.

Information shared with service providers

Purchases, through RevenueCat

If you start a trial or subscribe to Lento Pro, payment is handled by Apple; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created for your copy of Lento (not your name or email; Lento’s Profile shows it as your Support ID), your purchase and renewal history from Apple, Apple’s identifier for vendor (a device identifier that only apps from the same developer can read), and basic technical details such as app version, iOS version, device model, language, country and currency. We use this to unlock Lento Pro on your devices and to understand subscription revenue.

Usage analytics, through PostHog

We use PostHog, Inc. to understand how people use Lento, for example whether onboarding is completed, whether breathing sessions are finished, whether the subscription screen is viewed, and whether a purchase or a restore went through. These events are linked to the same random identifier (your Support ID), not to you, and include technical details such as app version, device model, iOS version, language and time zone. They never include your name, the apps you restrict, or your Screen Time data, and PostHog does not keep your IP address or work out your location from it. You can turn analytics off at any time in Lento’s Profile, under “Share anonymous usage data”.

Lento also reports, without naming the app: that a restriction was added (and from where: onboarding, Home or the + button) or removed, that a session started (its length in minutes and whether it was over budget), and that a session was ended early. Which apps you restrict, their names and your Screen Time never leave your iPhone.

Notifications

Lento’s reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone; no notification content passes through our servers.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, and no ad or tracking SDKs.
  • No tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • No accounts: Lento doesn’t ask for your email or password.

Why we use your information

Under UK GDPR we rely on two legal bases:

  • Contract: the purchase records needed to give you the Lento Pro you subscribed to.
  • Legitimate interests: anonymous usage analytics to improve Lento. You can object at any time by turning analytics off in Profile.

How long we keep it

Purchase records are kept for as long as you have a subscription with Lento and afterwards for as long as required for tax and accounting purposes. Analytics events are kept for no longer than 24 months. Information on your device stays until you delete it or the app.

International transfers

RevenueCat and PostHog may process data in the United States or the European Union. Where data leaves the UK, it is protected by the safeguards required by UK data protection law, such as the International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to its use. Because we don’t know who you are, please include the Support ID shown in Lento’s Profile when you email mertnesvat@gmail.com, so we can find your records. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Lento is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
