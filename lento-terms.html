Using Lento

Lento helps you build calmer habits with your phone. It is a wellbeing tool, not medical or psychological advice or treatment.

Lento depends on Apple’s Screen Time features. If Screen Time is turned off, restricted or unavailable on your device, Lento can’t restrict apps or websites. Please don’t rely on Lento in situations where access to an app is critical; you can always lift a restriction from Lento, or take Lento’s Screen Time access away in Settings.
